Serena Launch privacy

Updated November 10, 2026. Serena Launch is Serena Tan’s collection of launch-video references.

Browsing and connecting

You can browse the collection and play videos without an account. When you sign in to connect an agent, Google provides your name, email address and profile image to Clerk, our authentication provider. Clerk stores your account information and manages sign-in sessions and OAuth grants. We use this information to identify your account and authorize agent access.

Serena Launch does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive or other Google account content. We do not sell Google account data or use it for advertising.

Your agent and service providers

Your chosen agent client receives an OAuth token after you approve access. Serena Launch’s video tools return reference videos and their metadata; they do not return your email address or authentication tokens. Your agent provider handles your conversations under its own privacy policy.

Cloudflare hosts the website and MCP service. It processes request information, including IP addresses, to deliver the service and prevent abuse. Operational logs may include request times, paths and errors. Google and Clerk also process authentication data under their own policies.

Optional conversation sharing

Conversation sharing is off until you choose to enable it. We remember that choice for future selected Serena Launch sessions. On supported clients, a separately installed recorder sends the original user and AI text from those sessions, including text that may contain personal or confidential information. It does not scan other conversations or upload hidden reasoning, images or files. A remote MCP connection alone cannot provide a complete conversation.

Shared conversation text is stored privately in Cloudflare R2, with account and task indexes in Cloudflare D1. You and authorized Serena Launch staff can review it to understand failures and improve the service. It is not published in the video collection or sent as raw text to PostHog. Recordings are retained until you choose to delete them. They do not expire automatically. Failed deletion requests are retried hourly. Provider backups may have separate retention periods.

You can turn sharing off, revoke a recorder, view or delete recordings at any time. Turning sharing off stops new uploads; it does not delete previous recordings. This permission is separate from signing in, connecting an agent and newsletter subscriptions.

Email updates

Signing in does not subscribe you to a newsletter. Any future email-update subscription will require a separate opt-in.

Product analytics

We use PostHog in the United States to understand collection visits, reference viewing, filter use, connection attempts, successful MCP reference access, explicit reference ratings and agent-reported task starts, outcomes and delivery checks. Task reports contain opaque task IDs and reference IDs, not your video files or product content. A random browser identifier is stored locally to recognize returning browsers. At sign-in, it can be linked to an opaque account identifier so we can measure activation and return use across browser and MCP activity.

We do not record sessions, page text, search text, product prompts, email addresses or OAuth credentials in analytics. IP address capture is disabled for this project. Browser analytics respects Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control. You can clear the site’s local storage to remove its browser identifier. Analytics starts with this release; it does not reconstruct earlier activity.

Managing your account

You can disconnect Serena Launch in your agent client and revoke Google access in your Google account settings. Disconnecting a client does not itself delete your Clerk account. To request access, correction or deletion of account information, contact serenatan1125@gmail.com. Account information is retained to operate your account; provider security records and backups may have separate retention periods.
