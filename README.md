> A visual collection of launch videos worth studying — with playable MP4s, performance data, and copyable prompts to clone them 1:1 for your product with AI.

For founders, designers, and builders making their next product launch. Browse a style, watch a reference, then copy the prompt and make it yours.

**Every entry includes an inline video player, the video file, a remake prompt, and credit to the original creator.** The [live site](https://serenatan.org/launchvideo/) plays videos directly in the cards; all 74 high-resolution reference files are also included in [videos/](videos/).

Inspired by the visual + copyable-prompt format of [Awesome NotebookLM Templates](https://github.com/serenakeyitan/awesome-notebookLM-prompts).

## Use with your agent

```text
set up https://serenatan.org/launchvideo/agent/SKILL.md
```

[Connect Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Cursor, Grok & more](agent/README.md) · [Live MCP endpoint](https://serenatan.org/launchvideo/mcp) · [Plugin package](plugin/launch-archive)

The connector reads the current published library and workflow on every call. New videos and formats reach existing connections without reinstalling.

## How to use these prompts

1. Pick a video and copy its prompt, including the Twitter/X source link.
2. Paste it into your AI coding or video tool.
3. Add your product details and assets.

## Data & source notes

- Metrics are historical snapshots (Pacific time). Each entry records its own date; counts are not live. Unavailable counts appear as —.
- Impressions describe post exposure, not video plays. They do not establish organic reach or exclude paid promotion.
- Posting-account follower counts may describe a founder or employee rather than a company.
- Reference files use high-resolution source footage, with web-optimized H.264 encodes where needed. Eight entries are excerpts; their original durations are shown. Stats describe the full source posts.
- Style notes are based on sampled frames and supplied metadata. Style categories are editorial judgments.
- Full metadata, original Chinese notes, source links, and prompts are in [data/videos.json](data/videos.json).

## Contributing

Suggestions are welcome! Open a PR with a public source post, a preview, a useful style breakdown, and a copyable remake prompt. Include a dated source for any stats, and label excerpts clearly. Upload the MP4 as a GitHub video attachment and add its URL as `githubVideoUrl` in `data/videos.json`, then run:

```bash
python3 scripts/build_readme.py
```
